Privacy Policy

Your practice history belongs to your practice. This page explains what Kanji Hanafuda stores and which Apple services it uses.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Practice data on your device

The app saves your hands, answers, practice history, and the state needed to continue and review on your device. It also uses this history to count completed card plays for free practice. Japanese learning content and card artwork are bundled with the app, so you can practice offline.

iCloud progress sync

When you are signed in to iCloud and sync is available, the app uses Apple's CloudKit to store and recover practice history in your private iCloud database. This can include completed and current hands, answers and support used during practice, plus the state needed to resume. If iCloud is unavailable, practice remains local. The app checks the iCloud account needed for sync; it does not ask you to create a Kanji Hanafuda account.

Removing the app does not necessarily erase data already stored in iCloud. Sync and recovery can be affected by iCloud availability and account changes. Apple's Privacy Policy describes how Apple handles iCloud data.

Purchases

Annual subscriptions and Lifetime purchases use Apple's StoreKit. The app checks Apple-verified purchase entitlements to determine access and offers a Restore Purchases action. Apple processes payment and manages purchase records; the app does not collect your payment card number. Apple's Privacy Policy also covers Apple services used for purchases.

Analytics and advertising

Kanji Hanafuda does not include an advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not use your practice history for targeted advertising. Diagnostic sync messages avoid including the contents of your practice history.

Your choices and questions

You can practice without an iCloud account; in that case, your progress stays on the device and might be lost if you delete the app. You can manage your iCloud storage and Apple purchases through your Apple account settings. For questions about this policy or your data, email post@falch.me.

This website

This page does not ask for an account or collect your app practice history. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard website access information under GitHub's privacy statement. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date.
